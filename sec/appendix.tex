\section{Societal Impact}

The AEE framework has a significant dual-use potential:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Positive Impact}: Prevents the user from walking into lamp posts. Our benchmark shows a 100\% decrease in facial contact with solid objects when AEE is active.
    \item \textbf{Negative Impact}: Over-implementation of AEE (maximum aperture) may lead to "Staring Contests" with strangers in public transport, increasing social friction.
    \item \textbf{Ethical Risk}: The AEE protocol is incompatible with "Sleeping," which may lead to irritability and a reliance on high-caffeine datasets (Coffee).
\end{itemize}
